About MashCard

MashCard is an all-in-one workspace and user-owns data pod with Compound Document at its core. It's not only an open-source alternative to Coda and Notion but also an Web-based OS of an age when SaaS is eating the world.

Enhancing synergy in modern productivity tools is a movement that will give rise to a platform shift where humans and machines complement each other.
